Use case: ui-mockup. Product: 丸宠APP, a quiet original pet travel and collecting game for iOS. This is a HOME SCREEN visual concept, not an implemented app.
INPUT IMAGE 1 is the character-and-medium reference. Use that exact rounded calico cat identity, not the sheet layout: viewer-left apricot ear/forehead, viewer-right soft charcoal ear/cheek, cream-white paws and muzzle, tiny warm brown eyes, natural four-paw/seated feline shape. Wear the reference pale sage bandana. Preserve its silhouette, watercolor brush behavior, and asymmetrical patches.
Create realistic production-quality mobile UI design with clear hierarchy, strong readable Chinese typography, intentional watercolor imagery and purposeful spacing.
Target: one single tall portrait mobile app content frame, logical 390 x 844 pt, approximately 9:19.5 aspect ratio, render at high resolution. App content fills the image. No device bezel, no phone body, no rounded device mask, no status bar, no clock, no battery, no home indicator, no browser chrome, no external poster or presentation border. Do not combine screens.
Art: airy Japanese-inspired watercolor picture book, light warm-white paper with extremely subtle grain, transparent pale sage foliage, washed sky blue, tiny apricot accents, daylight. Gray-green text must stay dark and crisp, while illustration stays translucent. No sepia vintage parchment, no 3D, no neon, no gradient UI, no glossy cartoon style. Original character, architecture and interface; no existing franchise resemblance.
Scope and exact visible Chinese strings: brand "丸宠APP"; page "家园"; pet label "三花猫"; status "在家"; preparation status "行囊未准备"; one dominant action "准备行囊"; bottom navigation exactly "家园", "旅途", "收藏", with 家园 selected. These are the same contents in all home candidates. A small settings icon is permitted. No currency, counters, badges, quests, purchases, chat panels or invented feature cards. No invented pet name.
Body type around logical 16 pt, at most two typefaces, Chinese system-like clean rounded sans. Touch targets at least logical 44 pt. Prefer spacing and grouping over borders or shadows; no nested cards. Illustrations are richly crafted but UI remains sparse and legible. Leave breathing room around the primary action.
Current date anchor: 2026-10-17 Asia/Shanghai; do not show any dates or calendar.

Design direction name: 旅行手账.
Layout thesis: a light illustrated personal field journal, with a restrained scene in the upper half and a clearly organized spacious journal area in the lower half. This must be structurally distinct from a full-screen garden illustration and from a large framed room picture.
At top a small brand and settings row; a modest 家园 title. Upper roughly 45%: a loose, luminous watercolor vignette of the exact reference calico cat sitting on a pale blue cushion beside a low wood window ledge, a small houseplant and a glimpse of green landscape beyond. Sage bandana and off-white canvas travel satchel remain identical to the reference; hero cat about 30% of screen width. The scene feather-fades into the warm-white paper below; no visible rectangular picture border.
Lower roughly 40%: a carefully typeset airy travel-journal area, NOT many UI cards. Pet label 三花猫 on the left and small 在家 state on the right share one row. One ultra-light horizontal divider. Below a small independent watercolor satchel illustration and exact preparation status 行囊未准备; let this be one quiet focused readiness block with lots of white space. No extra narrative, no calendars, no quote, no diary entry, no inventory grid or fake trip history.
Primary 准备行囊 button is compact but wide and comfortable, solid muted sage with warm-white text, placed below the readiness block. Buttons and UI typography are cleaner than the watercolor art, with only a very subtle handmade edge. Add only a couple of very faint botanical pencil marks as margin decoration; do not add stickers, tape clutter or antique sepia paper.
Bottom navigation has the same three labels 家园 / 旅途 / 收藏, small calm line-and-watercolor icons, 家园 selected. This direction is lighter, whiter and more organized, with a smaller heading, less background detail and more visible breathing room than a scene-immersive game screen. Every text must be readable and align to a coherent mobile grid.
